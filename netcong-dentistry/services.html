01 — Preventive

Cleaning & Exam

Regular cleanings and exams are the foundation of a healthy smile. Our hygienists gently remove plaque and tartar, then polish your teeth to a fresh, smooth finish.

Every exam includes an oral-cancer screening and digital X-rays so we can spot small issues long before they become big ones.

  • Professional cleaning & polish
  • Full digital X-ray exam
  • Gum-health & cavity screening

02 — Urgent

Emergency Care

Tooth pain never waits for a convenient time — and neither do we. We reserve same-day appointments for cracked, knocked-out, abscessed or aching teeth.

Call us the moment something feels wrong and we will get you seen, comfortable and out of pain as quickly as possible.

Request an Appointment
03 — Oral surgery

Extractions

Extractions are performed to remove a tooth — whether because of disease, crowding or damage. When an extraction is required, the area around the tooth is fully numbed and your dentist gently removes the tooth.

A small amount of bleeding is normal afterward, and your mouth begins to heal as a protective blood clot forms in the area. We send you home with simple, clear aftercare instructions.

Request an Appointment
04 — Restorative

Fillings

Tooth-colored fillings repair cavities and restore the strength of a damaged tooth — without the dark look of old metal fillings.

We match the filling to the natural shade of your tooth, so your smile stays seamless and bright.

05 — Preventive

Preventive Dentistry

The healthiest smile is one that never needs major work. Sealants, fluoride treatments and personalized home-care plans help you and your kids avoid decay before it starts.

We will show you exactly what is working and where to focus — no lectures, just helpful guidance.

06 — Cosmetic

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening lifts years of coffee, tea and wine stains in a single, gentle visit — for a noticeably brighter smile you can see right away.

Prefer to whiten at home? Ask about our custom take-home trays for results on your own schedule.
