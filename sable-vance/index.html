About

The read between the lines

Sable Vance is a Los Angeles–based voice actor with a warm, smoky tone and an ear for the read between the lines. For over a decade she has lent her voice to global brands, streaming documentaries, animated series, and best-selling audiobooks.

Directable, fast, and obsessively clean, she treats every script like a performance — finding the honest, human read that makes a listener lean in. Sessions are collaborative, turnarounds are quick, and the audio arrives ready for air.
