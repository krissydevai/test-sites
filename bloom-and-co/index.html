04 — Our Story

A studio built on good hair days.

Bloom & Co started in 2019 with one chair, one mirror and a simple idea: hair should feel like you on your best day. No cookie-cutter trends pushed on everyone — just styling that suits your life.

Today we’re a tight crew in a sun-lit downtown studio, using bond-building, low-tox color and cuts designed to grow out gracefully. Come as you are, leave in full bloom.
